Sharing Your Trip Photos

Simple tips for getting your best photos from an educational or site inspection into our photo library, using the links you are sent.
Your links Which link to use Pick your top pics Uploading When to upload Checklist
📸

Your photos are worth a lot after the trip. They end up in quotes, presentations and marketing, so a great shot of a room or a view can help sell that hotel for years.

You don't need a login or an app. Before the trip you'll get a few links. Open the right one, add your photos, and they land in the right place for everyone to use.

1
Before you go

Your Links

1

What You'll Receive

How many links you get depends on the trip.

🧳 Educational with several stopsOne General link, plus one link for each hotel or activity along the way.
🏨 Single site inspectionUsually just one link for that property.

Save your links somewhere easy to find on your phone. For example, pin the message they came in, or save them in your notes, so they are one tap away on the trip.
2
On the trip

Which Link to Use

2

Hotel Link or General Link?

Hotel link: anything at that hotel, such as rooms, the pool, the restaurant, the spa and the views from the property.
General link: everything else, such as scenery between stops, the group, flights and transfers, markets and local life.
💡
Not sure which one? If it was taken at a hotel, use that hotel's link. If not, use General.
3

Pick Your Top Pics

Fewer great photos are far more useful than hundreds of similar ones. Before you upload, take a minute to choose your best.

👍 KeepClear, bright and straight photos. A good mix for each hotel: rooms, outside of the property, pool and views, food, and activities.
👎 Leave outBlurry or dark shots, near-identical repeats, and photos where a room looks untidy.
  • Take a photo of each room type you see. A quick snap of the room name on the door or info card makes it easy to tell them apart later.
  • Hold your phone sideways for rooms and views. These work best in presentations.
  • Upload the original photos from your phone, not copies saved from WhatsApp, as those lose quality.
4

Uploading

  1. Tap the link for the hotel, or the General link.
  2. If it asks for your name, add it, so we know whose photos they are.
  3. Choose your top pics and add them.
  4. Wait until the upload has finished before you close the page.
🔗
Link not opening, or upload not working? Let the person who sent you the links know. They can check it or send you a new one.
5

When to Upload

  • At the end of each day works best, while you still remember which photo was taken where.
  • Use the hotel Wi-Fi where you can, rather than your mobile data.
  • Don't wait until you're home. Photos from one hotel start to blur into the next very quickly.
